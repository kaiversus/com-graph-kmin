Design a modern, high-conversion Landing / Home Page for **COM Platform**, an IT learning and career development platform.

## Core Product Message

COM helps learners move from **learning to employment** through one connected journey:

**Learn → Practice → Build Projects → Get Mentored → Prove Skills → Build Portfolio → Get Hired**

COM is not just an online course platform. It is a **career development ecosystem** where every learning activity contributes to a learner’s Skill Graph, Portfolio, and Career Readiness.

The page should feel modern, credible, motivating, and career-oriented — suitable for university students, beginners, and junior IT professionals.

## 2. Career Journey / How COM Works

Show the complete learner journey as a horizontal or connected visual flow.

### Step 2 — Follow Your Roadmap

Receive a personalized learning path based on goals and skill gaps.

### Step 3 — Learn & Practice

Micro-learning content, quizzes, coding tasks, and practical exercises.

### Step 4 — Build Real Projects

Apply knowledge to projects inspired by real-world work.

### Step 5 — Learn With Mentors

Get project reviews, career advice, portfolio reviews, and skill validation.

### Step 6 — Prove Your Ability

COM continuously updates your Skill Graph and collects evidence.

### Step 7 — Build Your Career

Turn your learning journey into a public Portfolio and improve your Career Readiness.

Make the progression visually connected, not seven isolated cards.

# UX Principles

* Focus on learner outcomes rather than listing every feature.
* Each major section should communicate one clear benefit.
* Use product screenshots and UI mockups as primary visuals.
* Avoid large amounts of text.
* Maintain a strong visual rhythm between storytelling and product demonstration.
* Use subtle animations or connector lines to reinforce progress and relationships.
* The primary journey should always remain visible:
  **Learn → Build → Prove → Grow → Career**
* Design polished responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

---

# Visual Direction

Modern premium EdTech / SaaS style.

References for quality and visual discipline:

* Linear
* Notion
* GitHub
* Coursera
* Duolingo
* Vercel

Use:

* Clean typography
* Generous whitespace
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Rounded cards with subtle borders
* Minimal shadows
* Purposeful accent colors
* High-quality dashboard previews
* Graph visualization where useful
* Warm human elements for mentoring/community

Avoid:

* Generic stock-photo-heavy EdTech design
* Too many gradients
* Excessive cards
* Feature overload
* Cartoonish gamification
* Dense marketing copy

The final homepage should make a visitor understand within a few seconds:

**COM helps me know where I am, know what to learn next, build real evidence of my skills, and become ready for the job I want.**
